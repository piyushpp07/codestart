# CodeStart Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful community. Participate in good
faith, assume positive intent, and give feedback about ideas rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, or sharing another person's private data.

Project maintainers may remove, reject, or moderate contributions and comments
that violate these expectations. To report a concern privately, use the contact
method listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or the repository's private contact
channel once it is published.
